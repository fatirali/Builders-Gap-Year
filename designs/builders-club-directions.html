01 / How we learn

Learn by working through questions and projects.

01 —

The Socratic method

Explain your thinking and question the ideas you take for granted. Learn to change your mind when the evidence gives you a reason to.

02 —

Projects

Start with a problem people actually have. Build something they can try, then use their feedback to improve it.

03 —

Learning beyond your surroundings

Spend time in unfamiliar settings and get to know people who see things differently. Use what you learn in your projects.

02 / The program

What you’ll work on during the year.

Program phases

PHASE 1

Make your ideas clear.

Practice explaining your ideas in writing and in presentations. Learn to listen to your teammates and lead a project together.

What you’ll practice

  • Write about your ideas and the thinking behind them.
  • Present your work and listen to your teammates.
  • Practice coordinating a project with other people.

In practicePresent your ideas

PHASE 2

Take a product from idea to launch.

Use software and AI to develop a product, from choosing a problem to making a prototype and releasing it for people to use.

What you’ll practice

  • Talk to people about a problem they need to solve.
  • Build a prototype using software and AI.
  • Let people try it and improve it with their feedback.

In practiceLaunch a product

PHASE 3

Get to know other ways of working.

Explore how people in other cultures live and work. Learn to collaborate with people whose experiences differ from yours.

What you’ll practice

  • Explore how people live and work in other cultures.
  • Collaborate with people whose experiences differ from yours.
  • Bring what you learn back to your own projects.

In practiceCompare ways of working

Starting a business

Try an idea for a business.

Find out whether people need what you want to make. Work with your first users and learn what it takes to turn a project into a business.

Choosing a degree

Get a better idea of what you want to study.

Use the year to explore your interests through projects. That experience can help you decide what you’d like to study at university.
